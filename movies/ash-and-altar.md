# Ash and Altar

8-minute myth. Mia Cinema bible: `movies/ash-and-altar/` in the vault after 4.2.0 seeds.

## Story

A mother gives birth to a dragon. The village priest names her witch. She dies with the child under her coat.

The dragon grows in the hills. She could burn the square. She does not.

She lays her mother's iron pendant on the altar and waits until someone says the woman's name without the word witch. Rain. The people kneel. The dragon becomes the village's silent guardian.

Revenge would have made the priest right. Mercy is the ending.
